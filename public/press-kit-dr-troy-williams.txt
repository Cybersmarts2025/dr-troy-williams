BIOGRAPHY
==============================================

Dr. Troy Williams, PhD, is a cybersecurity engineer, AI scientist, and licensed private investigator with more than 30 years of experience in digital defense, fraud prevention, and sovereign AI development. His work aligns with the highest compliance standards, including GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, and CCPA. His research and publications are independently developed and published.
